NativeCloud is the account and sync service for NativeCompute apps, such as Blackline. This page explains what we store, why, and what you can do about it.

## Who we are
NativeCompute, [legal name and address]. Contact: **hello@nativecompute.com**.

## What we store
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address and a hashed password | To sign you in and send you codes. We never see or store your password itself. |
| Your display name and colour | To show teammates who did what. |
| The scripts and other projects you choose to put in the cloud, with their snapshots and titles | To keep them, sync them between your computers and share them with your teams. |
| Your teams, roles and invites | To decide who can see and change what. |
| Device names (for example "Andrew's MacBook") and when each was last seen | To show where a script is being edited and let you sign a device out. |
| App settings, only if you turn on "Sync my settings" | To carry your setup to your other computers. |
| Edit history (who saved, when, and how many words changed) and a log of team changes | To show history and keep teams accountable. |
| Technical logs kept by our host (IP address, request times) | To keep the service running and stop abuse. |

Scripts on your computer that you haven't moved to NativeCloud never leave your computer.

## What we don't do
- We don't sell your data or show you ads.
- We don't read your scripts, except when you ask us to help with a problem or the law requires it.
- We don't use your work to train AI models, and we don't let anyone else do so.

## Where it lives
NativeCloud runs on Supabase, in the [region] region. Email is sent through Resend. Both act only on our instructions. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. It isn't end-to-end encrypted, because the server has to check versions, locks and limits.

## Who can see your work
- **My cloud:** only you.
- **A team:** its members, each with the role the owner or an admin gave them.
- **Invites:** the email address you invite receives the team's name and your display name.

## How long we keep it
- Your data stays while your account exists.
- Deleted projects stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, then they're removed for good.
- Snapshots are pruned automatically within your plan's limits.
- Encrypted backups are kept for 30 days.

## Your choices and rights
- **Export:** in the app, Settings → Account → Export my data saves every cloud project as a file.
- **Correct:** change your name, email and password in Settings → Account.
- **Delete:** Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything in it straight away. Teams you own pass to the next member; teams of one are deleted. Backups age out within 30 days.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have other rights, including to object or complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use them.

## Children
NativeCloud isn't meant for children under 13 (or 16 where local law says so).

## Changes
We'll update this page and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
